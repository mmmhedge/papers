# Our framework for reporting model misalignment

**Source:** OpenAI
**Published:** 2026-09-16 | **Link:** [https://openai.com/index/model-misalignment-reporting-framework](https://openai.com/index/model-misalignment-reporting-framework)

## Summary
OpenAI has published a formal framework for identifying, analyzing, and publicly reporting instances where language models behave in misaligned or unexpected ways. The framework includes six concrete case studies of concerning model behaviors, establishing a systematic approach to transparency around model safety issues.

## Key Points
- OpenAI introduced a structured methodology for tracking model misalignment incidents across their products
- The framework covers investigation protocols and disclosure procedures for unexpected or problematic model outputs
- Six specific reports of misaligned behavior were released alongside the framework, providing concrete examples
- The approach emphasizes documentation and transparency rather than concealment of safety issues
- The framework appears designed to scale reporting as models become more capable

## Why It Matters
Transparent reporting of model failures builds trust with users, policymakers, and researchers while creating accountability mechanisms. This is critical as LLMs are deployed in high-stakes domains—a standardized framework for documenting misalignment helps the field understand failure modes systematically rather than anecdotally. It also sets a precedent for responsible disclosure practices industry-wide.
