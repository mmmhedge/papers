# Advancing computer use with Ironclad

**Source:** OpenAI
**Published:** 2026-10-06 | **Link:** [https://openai.com/index/advancing-computer-use-with-ironclad](https://openai.com/index/advancing-computer-use-with-ironclad)

## Summary
OpenAI and Ironclad are collaborating to train and evaluate AI agents capable of handling complex contracting workflows—real-world professional tasks that require reasoning, tool use, and document understanding. This work demonstrates progress toward autonomous agents that can perform meaningful business operations end-to-end.

## Key Points
- OpenAI and Ironclad partnered to apply AI agents to contract management, a domain requiring multi-step reasoning and domain expertise
- The collaboration involves both training agents on contracting workflows and developing evaluation methods for real-world task performance
- This represents a shift from narrow task automation toward broader professional workflows that traditionally required specialized human judgment
- The work combines language model capabilities with tool-use and planning to navigate complex, document-heavy processes

## Why It Matters
Demonstrating AI agents on professional workflows like contracting has significant implications: it validates that LLM-based agents can handle real business value (not just benchmarks), tests scalability to domains requiring specialized knowledge, and provides empirical grounding for evaluating agentic systems in production settings. Success here could accelerate adoption of AI agents across enterprise domains.
