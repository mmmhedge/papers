# Stochastic Dynamics on Persistence Diagram Space via Reinforcement Learning

**Authors:** Farzana Nasrin
**Published:** 2026-08-06 | **arXiv:** [2608.06276v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06276v1)

## Summary
Persistence diagrams (PDs) provide stable and interpretable summaries of multiscale topological structure. While substantial progress has been made in the statistical analysis of PDs, existing literature often treats diagrams as static objects and provide limited frameworks for probabilistic modeling and stochastic evolution on PD space. We introduce a reinforcement learning framework for stochastic dynamics on PD space, where diagrams evolve through topology aware local edit operations.

## Theoretical Background
Reinforcement learning trains a policy from rewards produced by interaction. In language-model training, reward quality and the match between training-time and deployment-time behavior are central constraints.

The paper is positioned against prior approaches described in its abstract and asks whether a more targeted representation, evaluation method, or optimization procedure can improve the relevant trade-off.
