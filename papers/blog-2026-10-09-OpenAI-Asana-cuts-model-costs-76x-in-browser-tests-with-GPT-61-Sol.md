# Asana cuts model costs 76x in browser tests with GPT-6.1 Sol

**Source:** OpenAI
**Published:** 2026-10-09 | **Link:** [https://openai.com/index/asana-browser-agent](https://openai.com/index/asana-browser-agent)

## Summary
Asana achieved a 76x reduction in model costs and 5x improvement in speed for its browser automation agent by using GPT-6 Astra integrated into Codex. This demonstrates significant efficiency gains in deploying agentic AI systems for real-world automation tasks at scale.

## Key Points
- Asana's browser agent now costs 76x less to operate using GPT-6 Astra in Codex
- Performance improved 5x faster in benchmark tests
- The optimization enables more capable AI models to be offered to customers while reducing operational costs
- This represents a practical deployment of advanced language models in agent-based automation

## Why It Matters
Cost and latency reductions of this magnitude directly affect the viability of agentic AI in production systems. Asana's use case suggests that latest-generation models can provide both better capabilities and lower costs, potentially accelerating adoption of browser automation and other agent-based workflows in enterprise software.
