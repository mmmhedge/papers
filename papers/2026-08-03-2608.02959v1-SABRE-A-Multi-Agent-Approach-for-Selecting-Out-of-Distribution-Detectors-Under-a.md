# SABRE: A Multi-Agent Approach for Selecting Out-of-Distribution Detectors Under a Budget

**Authors:** Mary Wisell, Salimeh Sekeh
**Published:** 2026-08-03 | **arXiv:** [2608.02959v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02959v1)

## Summary
Post-hoc out-of-distribution (OOD) detection for vision-language models assumes that a detector chosen on a benchmark stays reliable once deployed. We show this fails across domains: on a single frozen encoder, a detector that leads in one domain can invert in another, scoring in-distribution inputs as more anomalous than genuine outliers, and the best detector changes from domain to domain, so no fixed choice is reliable throughout. We introduce SABRE (Selective Agentic Budgeted Reliability Ensemble,) which replaces this fixed choice with per-regime selection at inference.
