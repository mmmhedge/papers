# Minimax-Optimal Semiparametric Contextual Dynamic Pricing with Multimodal Revenue

**Authors:** Xueping Gong, Zhuoluo Zhang, Zhaowei Miao, Jiheng Zhang
**Published:** 2026-08-04 | **arXiv:** [2608.03142v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03142v1)

## Summary
We study contextual dynamic pricing with arbitrary covariate sequences and bounded, possibly nonbinary purchase quantities. Demand follows a semiparametric surplus-index model with an unknown linear valuation parameter and an unknown Hölder-smooth response. We impose neither concavity nor strong unimodality on revenue and allow nonunique optimal prices.

## Core Contributions
1. We develop a pilot-corrected layered decision-partitioning policy that combines directional pilot estimation, local polynomial learning, predictable data assignment, and global action elimination.
