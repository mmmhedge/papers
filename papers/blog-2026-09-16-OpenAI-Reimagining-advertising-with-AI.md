# Reimagining advertising with AI

**Source:** OpenAI
**Published:** 2026-09-16 | **Link:** [https://openai.com/index/reimagining-advertising-with-ai](https://openai.com/index/reimagining-advertising-with-ai)

## Summary
OpenAI is launching AI-powered advertising products, including Sponsored Agents and marketer tools integrated with HubSpot and Shopify. This represents a shift toward embedding autonomous AI systems into commercial workflows to improve customer engagement and sales processes.

## Key Points
- **Sponsored Agents**: New AI agent capabilities designed for advertising and customer interaction use cases
- **Marketer Tools**: Purpose-built features to help marketing teams leverage AI for campaign creation and optimization
- **Platform Integrations**: Direct connections with HubSpot (CRM) and Shopify (e-commerce) to simplify deployment
- **Commercial Focus**: Signals OpenAI's expansion from language models into applied agent-based services for enterprise

## Why It Matters
This move demonstrates how frontier AI labs are monetizing and scaling LLMs through vertical applications. For businesses, it offers a pathway to deploy AI agents without building infrastructure from scratch. It also signals the industry shift from static generative models toward autonomous, goal-oriented systems operating in real commercial environments.
