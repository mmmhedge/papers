# Multimodal open d1 decision models for the edge

**Source:** Hugging Face
**Published:** 2026-10-07 | **Link:** [https://huggingface.co/blog/LiquidAI/open-d1](https://huggingface.co/blog/LiquidAI/open-d1)

## Summary
Hugging Face has released multimodal open decision models optimized for edge deployment. These models combine vision and language capabilities to enable real-time decision-making on resource-constrained devices without reliance on cloud infrastructure.

## Key Points
- Open-source multimodal models designed specifically for edge computing environments
- Integrate computer vision and language understanding for joint decision-making
- Focus on practical deployment constraints: latency, memory, and power efficiency
- Released as part of Hugging Face's effort to democratize capable AI systems

## Why It Matters
Edge deployment of decision models reduces latency, improves privacy, and enables offline operation—critical for robotics, autonomous systems, and real-time applications. Open-sourcing these models accelerates adoption and lowers barriers to building intelligent edge applications across industries.
