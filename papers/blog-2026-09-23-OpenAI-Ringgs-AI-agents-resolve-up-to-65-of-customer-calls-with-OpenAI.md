# Ringg’s AI agents resolve up to 65% of customer calls with OpenAI

**Source:** OpenAI
**Published:** 2026-09-23 | **Link:** [https://openai.com/index/ringg](https://openai.com/index/ringg)

## Summary
Ringg has deployed AI agents powered by GPT-5.6 that autonomously resolve up to 65% of customer service calls across voice, chat, WhatsApp, and web channels. The system operates at 90% lower cost than GPT-4.1 while supporting multilingual interactions, demonstrating practical scalability of agentic AI in customer support workflows.

## Key Points
- Ringg's agents handle customer calls end-to-end with a 65% resolution rate
- Supports multiple channels: voice, chat, WhatsApp, and web interfaces
- Multilingual capability across supported channels
- 90% cost reduction compared to GPT-4.1
- Built on GPT-5.6 architecture
- Indicates meaningful improvement in model efficiency and real-world agent autonomy

## Why It Matters
This deployment shows that LLM-based agents are moving from proof-of-concept to production at meaningful scale. The 65% autonomous resolution rate and dramatic cost savings suggest that routine customer service—a labor-intensive, high-volume problem—can be substantially automated, which has immediate business implications for cost reduction and efficiency gains across industries relying on customer support.

## Connections to Other Work
This relates to broader progress in [[Agents|agentic AI systems]] and the capability of [[Large Language Models|LLMs]] to operate autonomously across diverse modalities. It builds on earlier work in voice-based conversational AI and extends [[NLP|natural language processing]] into production customer-facing applications with measurable business metrics.

Ringg's GPT-5.6 agents resolve 65% of customer calls at 90% lower cost than prior models, moving agentic AI into production deployment.
