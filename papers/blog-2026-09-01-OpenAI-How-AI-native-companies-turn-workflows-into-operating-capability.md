# How AI-native companies turn workflows into operating capability

**Source:** OpenAI
**Published:** 2026-09-01 | **Link:** [https://openai.com/index/ai-native-company-workflows](https://openai.com/index/ai-native-company-workflows)

## Summary
This post examines how companies like Basis, Clay, and Exa Labs use AI agents to automate and optimize business workflows—specifically onboarding, account management, and developer integrations. The article distills practical patterns that enterprise organizations can adopt to turn AI capabilities into measurable operational improvements.

## Key Points
- Three case studies demonstrate AI agents handling customer onboarding, account lifecycle management, and API integration workflows
- AI-native companies treat agents as core infrastructure, not as one-off automation tools
- Practical applications include reducing manual handoffs, improving customer experience, and lowering operational friction
- The pattern appears generalizable across onboarding, support, and developer-facing operations

## Why It Matters
As AI agents mature, their business value shifts from experimentation to operational embedding. Organizations that systematize agent deployment across workflows gain competitive advantage through faster execution and reduced human overhead. This post provides concrete templates for enterprises moving beyond chatbots to agentic automation.
