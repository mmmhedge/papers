# Scientific computing in the age of agentic AI

**Source:** OpenAI
**Published:** 2026-07-28 | **Link:** [https://openai.com/index/scientific-computing-agentic-ai](https://openai.com/index/scientific-computing-agentic-ai)

## Summary
OpenAI reports on how AI coding agents are being deployed in scientific research to accelerate software development and discovery workflows. The field report demonstrates practical applications in genomics and other domains where agents handle code generation and iteration tasks that previously required manual engineering effort.

## Key Points
- AI coding agents are being used to modernize scientific computing pipelines
- Documented applications span genomics and related computational fields
- The agents accelerate both software development cycles and scientific discovery
- This represents a shift toward agentic AI handling complex, iterative technical tasks in research

## Why It Matters
Agentic AI could significantly reduce friction in scientific software development, allowing researchers to focus on hypothesis generation and interpretation rather than engineering. This could democratize access to advanced computational methods and compress timelines for experimental validation in biology and other data-intensive fields.
