## Summary
OpenAI shares internal data on how coding agents are accelerating their research workflow. The post presents metrics on agent adoption, experiment velocity, task complexity, and overall research productivity gains from deploying AI agents for research tasks.

## Key Points
- Coding agents are being used operationally within OpenAI to speed up research cycles
- Early data tracks agent usage patterns, showing adoption across research teams
- Experiment velocity has increased—agents enable faster iteration on research hypotheses
- Task complexity is being tackled by agents, suggesting they handle non-trivial research problems
- Research acceleration is measurable; OpenAI is quantifying productivity gains from agent deployment

## Why It Matters
This represents a shift where AI systems are directly contributing to AI research itself, creating a potential feedback loop of acceleration. It demonstrates that agents are moving beyond prototypes into production research workflows, which has implications for how quickly capabilities can improve and how research timelines may compress. For the field, this suggests agent-assisted research may become standard practice.
