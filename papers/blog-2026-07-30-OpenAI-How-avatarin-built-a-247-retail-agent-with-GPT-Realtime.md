# How avatarin built a 24/7 retail agent with GPT-Realtime

**Source:** OpenAI
**Published:** 2026-07-30 | **Link:** [https://openai.com/index/avatarin](https://openai.com/index/avatarin)

## Summary
Avatarin deployed a real-time conversational agent powered by OpenAI's GPT-Realtime to provide 24/7 multilingual customer support at Yamada Denki retail stores. The system reached 30,000 users in two weeks with a 92% positive satisfaction rate, demonstrating practical viability of LLM-based retail agents in production.

## Key Points
- GPT-Realtime enabled low-latency, natural conversations in multiple languages for retail customer service
- Yamada Denki shoppers accessed the agent continuously without human staff constraints
- 30,000 users engaged with the system within a two-week pilot window
- 92% of survey respondents reported satisfaction with the service
- The deployment suggests commercial viability of real-time agentic systems in e-commerce and brick-and-mortar retail

## Why It Matters
This case study validates that LLM agents can handle real-world customer service at scale with strong user satisfaction, reducing operational costs while improving availability. It signals that real-time inference is now viable for production consumer applications, shifting the incentive structure toward agentic architectures in retail and hospitality.

## Key Takeaway
Real-time LLM agents delivered high-satisfaction 24/7 retail support to 30,000 users, proving commercial viability at scale.
