# CoPlan: A Trustworthy Co-Intelligence Interface for Care Planning through Role-Based Contestable Argument Graphs

**Authors:** Hung Truong Thanh Nguyen, Hélène Fournier, Piper Jackson, Makoto Itoh, Shannon Freeman et al.
**Published:** 2026-08-05 | **arXiv:** [2608.05107v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05107v1)

## Summary
AI-supported care planning can help clinicians, patients, caregivers, and care teams coordinate complex decisions across clinical, functional, psychosocial, and environmental needs. However, many AI systems present recommendations as fixed outputs, limiting stakeholders' ability to inspect, challenge, and revise plans when they conflict with clinical judgment, patient values, or real-world feasibility. We present CoPlan - a Co-Intelligent and Contestable Interface for Human-AI Care Planning.

## Theoretical Background
High-stakes predictive systems require traceable inputs, calibrated uncertainty, and evidence that results generalize beyond development data.
