# SIGNPOST-Bench: Benchmarking Text-Vision Conflict Resolution in Multimodal Large Language Models

**Authors:** Sirun Li, Minghao Liu, Ling Dai, Yong Li, Haoxin Lyu et al.
**Published:** 2026-08-04 | **arXiv:** [2608.04244v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04244v1)

## Summary
Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) make grounded predictions in real-world scenes by combining visual and textual cues, yet existing benchmarks rarely reveal how they arbitrate between these evidence sources when they conflict. We introduce SIGNPOST-Bench, a controlled counterfactual benchmark for evaluating text-vision conflict resolution. Each source image is transformed into a counterfactual quintuplet of Original, Blank, Similar, Random, and Adversarial variants.

## Theoretical Background
Language systems must represent meaning, retrieve evidence, and follow task constraints. Evaluation should separate these capabilities rather than relying on one aggregate score.

The paper addresses the trade-off identified in its abstract through a targeted method, representation, or evaluation framework.

## Core Contributions
1. We introduce SIGNPOST-Bench, a controlled counterfactual benchmark for evaluating text-vision conflict resolution.
2. Synthetic, localized scene-text interventions are designed to preserve non-textual content, enabling paired measurements of changes in localization performance and directed shifts toward geographic targets introduced by conflicting text.
3. We evaluate 20 MLLMs from seven providers.
