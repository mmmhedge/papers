# From assistance to execution: How enterprises put AI to work

**Source:** OpenAI
**Published:** 2026-08-12 | **Link:** [https://openai.com/index/how-enterprises-put-ai-to-work](https://openai.com/index/how-enterprises-put-ai-to-work)

## Summary
OpenAI reports on how enterprises are transitioning from using AI for assistance to deploying agentic AI systems for autonomous execution of tasks. The research shows that frontier companies are adopting ChatGPT and code generation tools at scale, creating a widening gap between early adopters and laggards in AI capability deployment.

## Key Points
- Enterprises are moving beyond chatbots and assistants toward agentic AI that can execute tasks autonomously
- ChatGPT and Codex are being used as foundational tools for enterprise AI adoption
- Frontier firms are pulling ahead competitively through more aggressive and sophisticated AI integration
- The shift from "assistance" (human-in-loop) to "execution" (autonomous agents) represents a maturation in enterprise AI deployment

## Why It Matters
This signals a critical inflection point in enterprise AI adoption. As agentic systems move from research prototypes to production workloads, companies gain measurable competitive advantages in automation, efficiency, and cost reduction. The widening adoption gap also suggests that organizational capability and willingness to deploy autonomous systems—not just access to models—is becoming a key differentiator.
