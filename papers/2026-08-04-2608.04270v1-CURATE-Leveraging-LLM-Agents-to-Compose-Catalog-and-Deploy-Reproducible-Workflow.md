# CURATE: Leveraging LLM Agents to Compose, Catalog, and Deploy Reproducible Workflows

**Authors:** Nolan Cutler, Chia-Chen Kuo, Nanda Velugoti, Kathryn Newhart, Renato Figueiredo
**Published:** 2026-08-04 | **arXiv:** [2608.04270v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04270v1)

## Summary
Agentic code generation has shown promise in automating and accelerating software development by utilizing Large Language Models (LLMs) to generate, test, and deploy code. For engineers and scientists, such systems have the potential to accelerate the development of applied and scientific workflows while reducing barriers to entry in domains that have yet to fully realize their benefits. However, a key gap remains: existing coding agents primarily focus on code generation and do not address the entire workflow lifecycle, including deployment and sharing.

## Theoretical Background
AI agents combine models with tools, memory, and execution loops. Reliability therefore depends on decisions across a trajectory, not only the quality of isolated answers.

The paper addresses the trade-off identified in its abstract through a targeted method, representation, or evaluation framework.
