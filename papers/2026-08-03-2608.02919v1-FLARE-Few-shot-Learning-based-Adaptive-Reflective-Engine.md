# FLARE: Few-shot Learning-based Adaptive Reflective Engine

**Authors:** Dhanasekar Sundararaman, Bharat Gandhi, Aashna Garg, Minjie Li
**Published:** 2026-08-03 | **arXiv:** [2608.02919v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02919v1)

## Summary
Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly deployed in complex, compound AI systems where performance hinges on the quality of prompts. Recent state-of-the-art optimizers like GEPA (Genetic-Pareto) have argued that reflective instruction evolution can outperform traditional reinforcement learning and few-shot optimization. In this work, we challenge this shift by introducing FLARE (Few-shot Learning-based Adaptive Reflective Engine), a framework that leverages advanced reflective mechanisms and a small set of few-shot reference examples to optimize instructions.
