# AS-FedBridge: Pseudo-Spike Bridge Distillation for Heterogeneous ANN-SNN Federated Learning

**Authors:** Shengyang Li, Yiting Dong, Liuyang Song, Ximing Wang, Luyuan Xie et al.
**Published:** 2026-08-04 | **arXiv:** [2608.03324v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03324v1)

## Summary
Federated learning enables collaborative model training across distributed edge devices while strictly preserving data privacy. To facilitate practical deployment on resource-constrained edge devices, Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs) have emerged as a promising alternative to traditional Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) due to their sparse computing mechanisms and high energy efficiency. However, jointly training ANNs and SNNs exposes a challenge of representational misalignment, which is intrinsically caused by differences in information representation, specifically the semantic gap between continuous real-valued activations in ANNs and discrete spatio-temporal spikes in SNNs.

## Core Contributions
1. However, jointly training ANNs and SNNs exposes a challenge of representational misalignment, which is intrinsically caused by differences in information representation, specifically the semantic gap between continuous real-valued activations in ANNs and discrete spatio-temporal spikes in SNNs.
2. To overcome this barrier, we propose AS-FedBridge, a novel federated learning framework tailored for mixed ANN-SNN clients.
3. Given the absence of existing mixed ANN-SNN federated frameworks, we establish a comprehensive benchmark to evaluate against multiple advanced heterogeneous FL methods.
